## How to read this

- **Isolation is the only hard gate that matters commercially.** A single cross-tenant leak invalidates the product regardless of retrieval quality.
- **Recall@5 is the primary quality metric.** A chunk not retrieved cannot be recovered by any downstream LLM.
- **Entity linking rate near zero means the graph path is dead** and every query is silently falling through to vector search.
- **Fallback rate is the early-warning signal.** A rising trend means retrieval is degrading before users report it.
- **Graph lift is the architectural justification.** If multi-hop questions score the same with and without traversal, the graph is cost without benefit.
